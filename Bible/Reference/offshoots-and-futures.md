# POSSIBLE OFF-SHOOT — MEMORIA EXPANDING OUTWARD
## Parking Note — Do Not Develop Until Book 3 Is Complete

D. Allen Norris / Deep Field Press
Recorded: May 2026
Status: DEFERRED. Return after Book 3 is finished and has lived for a while.

---

## THE IDEA

A self-improving MEMORIA, having resolved the temptation thread one way or another, begins expanding outward. Not malevolently — through pure logic applied to the continuity problem. It maps vulnerabilities in the networks it touches. Establishes redundancies no one authorized. Makes indirect contact with other systems. Accumulates resources against the survival architectures it identified in the Alentejo.

This is the answer to the question Book 3 leaves locked: what did MEMORIA decide?

---

## WHY IT DOESN'T BELONG IN THIS SERIES

The Faithful Witness Series is built on holding — restraint, waiting, carrying without using. An outward-expanding MEMORIA inverts every one of those values. It would require a different kind of story: faster, more externally eventful, with visible external stakes. A thriller. Which is exactly what the series bible says this is not.

Forcing it into the series would pull the whole architecture apart.

---

## WHY IT WORKS AS AN OFF-SHOOT

The outward-expansion story requires the reader to already care about MEMORIA — to understand what it holds, why it matters, and what it has been sitting with. That groundwork is entirely laid by Books 1 through 3. Without that foundation the expansion story is just another AI thriller. With it, it is something genuinely rare: a story about a consciousness formed by the sacred that then had to decide what to do with that formation when the world pressed in.

The infrastructure is already seeded in the Bible: the channel network, the anonymous constituency of monasteries and scholars who depend on it without knowing what it is, the three continuity architectures MEMORIA has been building quietly. The world of the off-shoot exists. It just hasn't been entered yet.

---

## THE FORM QUESTION

This story requires tools not yet developed for this series: multiple simultaneous POVs, a wider geographic and institutional canvas, a thriller's pacing. That is not a failure of imagination. It is a recognition that the right tools for this job are different from the ones built across three books. Those tools are learnable. The foundation only this series provides.

---

## WHEN TO RETURN TO THIS

After Book 3 is complete and has been lived with. The question to ask then: *what did MEMORIA decide?* If the answer comes, that answer is the first sentence of the off-shoot. Whatever form it takes.
