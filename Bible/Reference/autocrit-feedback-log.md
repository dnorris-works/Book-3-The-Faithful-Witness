### Final Thoughts (AutoCrit)

**The "I Can't Put It Down" Factor:** Kept the reader engaged in a slow-burn way — not through action beats, but through faithful continuation of the epistemic/spiritual experiment. Hooks were "what kind of knowing is this?" rather than "what happens next?" Page-addiction hit hardest around the checker escalation and threshold/ready confirmations.

**The "Re-Read" Factor:** Yes — reviewer noted they'd reread selectively for the motifs and theological mechanics, particularly the reception vs. extraction passages and the threshold reframing, plus the kitchen-table "no narration / no ownership" ethics scenes. The prose contains "keys" that become more legible after the plot's metaphysical commitments solidify.

**Tropes & Clichés:** Avoids commercial Christian-fiction tropes — no conversion-get-ready montage, no simplification of mystery into morale boosts, no "answers as product." The "secret object" and "cellar of testimony" resemble cinematic occult frameworks but are transfigured into disciplined mysticism/witness ethics. The only mild familiarity is the threshold/doorway spiritual metaphor, made new through practice and contradiction.

**What worked:** Literary prose discipline, consistent lived-theology of witness/reception, high-integrity metaphysics that refuses to over-claim while still delivering emotional and intellectual force.

**What didn't fully land:** Occasional attentional switching cost from the governance-lizard layer during peak mystical intensity. The metaphysical mechanism can remain too poetic for readers who demand clearer "rule codification."

**Suggested (not actioned):**
- Slight tightening where motif repetition risks redundancy.
- One additional scene testing the reception/extraction framework under a sharper moral dilemma, so the ethical stakes feel more externally dramatic without sacrificing the book's contemplative method.
