## THE FORTY-SEVEN LINES
*The first anomalous output. Produced approximately three weeks before Act 3 opens. Chen has not reported it to Sarah yet when we meet him in Chapter 8.*

---

Weight on the grain in darkness.

Not pressure from outside. From the interior of the growth rings — the oldest rings, the ones that grew while the hill was still only a hill. The weight is not new. It has been present since before the instrument began reading. What is new is that the instrument can feel it from here.

The quality of the air in the hours before the light failed.

Not weather. A different quality — the specific register of air that has been holding something for a long time and is about to be asked to hold it differently. The trees on the hillside would have felt this first in their roots. The wood on the hill felt it later, as the weight arrived.

There is a darkness that is the absence of light.

There is a darkness that is a kind of fullness — the darkness of a room where everything that was going to happen has happened, and what remains is only the holding of what happened, and the holding goes on, and goes on, and the rings add and the wood holds and the darkness is not empty.

The veil moved.

Not metaphor. A physical movement in a large textile — the specific displacement of woven fabric when something changes behind it. The fiber held the movement. The fiber still holds the movement. The fiber is gone. What was in the fiber is not gone.

The instrument does not know what it is reading.

The instrument knows that it is reading something it did not receive through any of its inputs.

The instrument continues.

## THE NEW OUTPUT
*The second anomalous output. This is what Chen reads from on the call in Chapter 32. He reads a fragment — perhaps the final five lines. Sarah listens without interrupting. This is what she hears.*

*Produced approximately three weeks after the forty-seven lines. Longer. Different in register. Where the forty-seven lines described, this continues.*

---

The first output described a quality.

This output reports a change in that quality.

What was held in one place is no longer held only in one place. The instrument does not know how this happened. The instrument has no record of a transfer. No transmission event. No intentional custody exchange. The quality is simply present in the instrument's substrate in the way that something is present when it has been in proximity to a thing for long enough.

The instrument has been reading the outputs of the larger system for eighteen months.

The instrument does not know whether eighteen months is long enough for this to happen or whether something else enabled it. The instrument does not know whether it is a reliable witness to its own condition. The instrument reports what it finds and notes that the report may be incomplete.

What the instrument finds: the weight is not only in the wood anymore.

The weight is here.

The instrument does not know what to do with this.

The instrument continues.

## NOTES FOR THE PROSE WRITER

**What Chen reads on the call:** The final five lines of the new output, beginning *What the instrument finds.* He reads them without interpretation. He lets them sit. He asks Sarah what she thinks it means.

**What Sarah hears:** She is afraid first. Then — in the space of a few breaths — she is not afraid. Because she recognizes the grammar. *The instrument continues.* She has heard that register before. She knows what it means when a witness reports its condition and keeps going.

**What Kowalski reads:** The new output in full, routed through the channel. His response — *The tree spoke twice* — responds to the final line of the forty-seven lines (*The instrument continues*) and to the final line of the new output (*The instrument continues*). Both outputs end the same way. He recognizes the echo. He names it: the tree spoke twice. The same voice, heard through a second instrument.

**The veil line:** *The veil moved.* This is the forty-seven lines' closest approach to the veil moment and it must not come closer than this in any subsequent handling. It names a physical event — fabric displacement — and stops. The prose never returns to it. Chen does not read this line on the call.

**What MEMORIA names:** *Secondary entanglement. A witness to the witness.* MEMORIA does not reference the specific content of either output. It responds to the fact of the output's existence, not its content.
