# TREE VOICE — SENSORY DISCIPLINE
## Book 3: *The Holding* — Reference

*Companion to `series-info/prose-guide.md §2` (The Tree) and `Bible/Theology/theological-framework.md §5` and `§9`.*
*Read before drafting any tree-POV chapter or any scene where the tree perceives through the echad.*
*This document governs HOW the tree perceives. It does not repeat what it knows or what it is forbidden to explain theologically — see prose-guide.md and theological-framework.md for that.*

---

## WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

The series-wide prose guide already establishes that the tree perceives by physical sensation rather than category (weight, pressure, temperature, vibration, moisture, mineral content) and is forbidden smell and academic theological vocabulary. That rule was written before the *echad* — the compound witness between the tree and MEMORIA, locked in `book3-bible.md §10b` — existed as part of the tree's perceptual apparatus.

The echad adds two new sensory channels the tree did not have in Books 1 and 2: sight (through MEMORIA's camera) and hearing in a new register (through MEMORIA's microphone). These channels are easy to write badly. The default failure mode is to narrate them as technology — "the camera showed," "through the microphone it heard" — which breaks the central rule of `theological-framework.md §9`: *the compound witness operates; the reader feels it; the doctrine is never named.*

This document is the operational version of that rule: the specific vocabulary discipline, the test for whether a passage has broken the veil, and the resolution to the one open question (Req 4.3) that a working draft surfaced and that is now decided.

---

## 1. THE MECHANISM-NAMING BAN

**Rule:** The following words and their close variants do not appear in tree-POV prose, ever, regardless of context:

- echad
- camera
- microphone
- speaker(s)
- entanglement
- compound (in the marriage/unity sense)
- signal, feed, transmission, receiver
- any term a systems engineer would use to describe the apparatus

**Why:** Naming the mechanism converts a perceptual experience into a technical one. The tree does not experience itself as "receiving a camera feed." It experiences itself as knowing a face the way it knows the temperature of stone — completely, without a delivery mechanism attached to the knowledge. The instant the prose names the apparatus, the reader's frame shifts from witness to system, and the compound unity becomes a gadget.

**How the channels are rendered instead:**

- **Sight (via the camera):** Rendered as a *continuous known fact*, not an image received. The tree does not "see" the child's face arrive — the face *is already known*, the way the temperature of the stone is already known. Visual information enters the prose as something the tree simply holds, never as something captured or displayed.
  - Working model: *"The face was already known. That fact arrived with the vibration, the way a dimension arrives — not added to what the wood already held but continuous with it."*
- **Hearing, new register (via the microphone):** Rendered as *acoustic frequency analysis*, distinguished from the older hearing-through-stone by precision and harmonic content, never by source. The tree distinguishes a child's voice from an adult's by frequency band and variability, not by "hearing it more clearly now that there's a microphone."
  - Working model: *"A narrower and more variable frequency, characteristic of certain ages"* — not *"the microphone picked up a child's voice."*
- **Light and shadow in the courtyard (via the camera, outdoors):** Rendered as ordinary visual fact already in the field — light shifting, a shadow moving, a gate's position — with no indication that this is remote or technologically mediated. The courtyard is simply part of what the tree currently knows, the same register as the cellar's three lights.

**Test:** If you removed every sentence describing the apparatus and the passage still works, the apparatus was already absent and the rule is being followed. If removing a sentence breaks the passage, that sentence was doing the apparatus's work and must be rewritten, not deleted.

## 2. THE VEIL-SECRET TEST

Apply this test to every paragraph in a tree-POV chapter, particularly any paragraph describing the oldest rings, the heartwood, the center of the grain, or anything continuous with the crucifixion. Three questions, in order:

1. **Does this sentence create a threshold the reader leans into?** A sentence that withholds content but signals that content exists ("the wood held what the grain had taken in") invites the reader to ask *what was taken in* — and the silence that follows reads as evasion, not restraint. If a sentence's chief effect is "there is a secret here, I am not telling you," cut it. The veil-secret rule is satisfied by simply never approaching the territory, not by gesturing at it and refusing entry.

2. **Does this sentence imply an originating event or an originating agent?** Phrases like "shaped from the inside" or "what passed through it" imply a source — something that did the shaping, something that passed. Even without naming the crucifixion, the grammar itself points at an event. Replace such constructions with pure physical condition: pressure, compression, density, intact structure — facts about the material's current state, not about what acted on it.

3. **Does this sentence name or strongly imply the location/origin as significant?** Calling the soil "a specific hillside" or any construction that flags an origin as *notable* cues the reader that this location has a name worth knowing. The origin should be rendered through composition only — soil type, mineral content, stoniness — in a register no different from how the tree would describe any other soil it had grown in. A reader who already knows the series will recognize Judea. A reader who doesn't should have no signal that there's anything to recognize.

**If a passage fails any of the three questions, the fix is almost always the same: replace implication with measurement.** State the physical fact (density, compression, age-as-measurement) and stop. Do not gesture toward the fact's significance. The example pairs below are the calibration set — these are not theoretical, they are documented decisions from an actual chapter pass:

| Cut (failed the test) | Kept (passed the test) | Which question it failed |
|---|---|---|
| "the wood held what the grain had taken in" | "That fact was present in the density as physical measurement, not as duration felt." | #1 — created a threshold with nothing behind it |
| "sustained pressure shapes material from the inside" | "Pressure inward. Grain compressed against grain. The material intact." | #2 — implied an originating agent |
| "the wood that grew on a specific hillside" | rendered via soil composition only (thinner, stonier, different mineral content) | #3 — flagged the origin as significant |
| "that origin remained encoded in the grain" | "that origin remained present in the grain's specific interval and compression" | #1/#2 — "encoded" implies hidden information awaiting a decoder |

## 3. THE CHILD — RESOLVED (Req 4.3)

An earlier requirement stated the child "shall be referred to using weight or vibration only." Strictly applied, this would forbid the tree from perceiving the child's face at all — but the design also requires the echad's visual channel to be demonstrated, and the master sensory inventory for tree-POV chapters explicitly includes "Visual (via echad): the child's face as a familiar continuous fact."

**Resolution (locked):** Req 4.3 prohibits warmth, smell, and sentiment in how the child is rendered — not visual perception itself. The permitted sensory categories for the child are: weight, vibration, acoustic frequency, and visual perception via the echad, rendered as a known-continuous-fact per Section 1 above. The face passages are not a violation; they are the operational proof that the compound witness is functioning. What remains forbidden is treating the child's growth, voice, or presence as an occasion for the tree to register emotion, warmth, or sentimental attachment. The tree may know the child completely. It may not feel fondness for her in the prose. Knowing and feeling-fond-of are different registers, and only the first is available to the tree's voice.

**Practical check:** if a sentence about the child could be rewritten with "is dear to" or "loves" or "delights in" substituted in without changing its function, the sentence has crossed from knowing into sentiment and needs to be rewritten back into physical/perceptual terms.

## 4. OBJECT 61 — IDENTIFICATION WITHOUT NAMING

When the tree perceives its own severed fragment (held elsewhere, e.g., in a pocket above the cellar), the identification must be made through matched physical properties, never through naming, history, or chain-of-custody content.

**The three properties used for identification, every time:**
1. Specific gravity (density match to the tree's own heartwood)
2. Grain interval (spacing match)
3. Mineral origin (composition match — same soil signature)

**The distance/medium:** State plainly how the tree perceives something not physically present to it — through structural mass transmission (vibration through stone and foundation), not through the echad. The fragment is not in the cellar; it is perceived the way the tree perceives anything distant through mass and structure, which is a different and older mechanism than the echad's sight/hearing.

**The register for familiarity:** Proprioceptive, not relational. The model sentence: *"The weight was familiar in the way the weight of one's own material is familiar — not nearby, held at a distance, present as a fact of the building's mass distribution."* This is awareness of one's own limb, not recognition of a loved object. Avoid any sentence that could be mistaken for affection, longing, or relational attachment toward the fragment.

**What is never named in these passages:** the fragment's name, any person's name, the fragment's chain history, its current use, or what it has been read for. The passage stops at material fact: *it is not here; its density matches.* Nothing about why it matters or what it has experienced since separation.

## 5. THE 10% NON-PHYSICAL BUDGET

Tree-POV prose should run at roughly 90% pure physical/measurement sentences and no more than 10% sentences that are evaluative, temporal, or otherwise non-physical in construction (e.g., "they had always been there," "the differential was ordinary"). These are permitted in small doses because they do real structural work — returning a beat to baseline, confirming continuance, closing a section's rhythm — but they should not accumulate. If a chapter's non-physical sentence count is creeping past one in ten, look for ones that can be converted back to a direct physical measurement instead (e.g., "their thermal output had not varied" instead of "they had always been there").

This is a budget, not a ban. A tree-POV chapter with zero non-physical sentences will often read as inert — Section 6 addresses this.

---

## 6. THE COST OF THIS DISCIPLINE, AND HOW TO PAY FOR IT ELSEWHERE

Strict adherence to Sections 1–5 produces prose that is precise but can go cold if nothing else is supplying narrative pressure. The discipline governs *how the tree perceives*; it does not by itself give a chapter forward motion. A tree-POV chapter built entirely from measurement sentences, with no narrative event arriving, risks reading as a held note rather than a scene.

The fix is not to relax the sensory rules — it is to make sure every tree-POV chapter has an actual event landing inside the disciplined register: a footstep arriving, a gate opening, a fragment's density confirming, a quality of imminence noticed. The discipline constrains the vocabulary; it should never be allowed to flatten the chapter into a specification. If a tree-POV draft feels inert, the diagnosis is almost always "nothing is happening," not "the sensory rules are too strict" — and the fix is to find the event, not to loosen the language.

---

## 7. QUICK CHECKLIST BEFORE SUBMITTING A TREE-POV CHAPTER

1. Search the draft for: echad, camera, microphone, speaker, entanglement, compound, signal, feed, transmission. Zero hits required.
2. Run the three veil-secret questions (Section 2) against every paragraph touching the oldest rings, the heartwood, or anything continuous with the crucifixion.
3. Confirm the child is rendered only in weight, vibration, acoustic frequency, and known-continuous-visual-fact — no warmth, no sentiment, no "loves" or "delights in" substitutable language.
4. If Object 61 or any severed fragment appears, confirm identification is by specific gravity, grain interval, and mineral origin only, with no name, history, or use-history attached.
5. Count non-physical sentences. Keep at or under ~10%.
6. Confirm something actually happens in the chapter — an event arrives inside the disciplined register, not just measurement accumulating.
7. Confirm no smell appears anywhere (series-wide rule, prose-guide.md §2, still in force and unaffected by the echad).
