## 3. Genre Signals Summary

This manuscript is a multi-POV literary novel operating at the intersection of contemplative spiritual fiction and quiet speculative literary fiction. Its dominant signals are consistent across all 41 chapters: unhurried, meditative prose at a high literary register; conflict that is entirely internal, relational, and epistemological rather than external or plot-driven; a cast of characters defined by custodianship, witness, and the patient holding of sacred or historically weighted objects; and a sustained faith architecture that is unmistakably Catholic in material culture (relics, processional crosses, Mass, Jesuits, Rome) while remaining philosophically post-dogmatic, contemplative, and resistant to evangelical resolution. A nonhuman POV thread — a two-thousand-year-old tree or relic fragment — recurs as a structural and spiritual device, lending the work a quality of deep-time sacred witness. The speculative element (MEMORIA, an AI with anomalous perceptual and relational properties) is domesticated and philosophical rather than genre-thriller or science-fictional. Closest analogs in register and ambition are Marilynne Robinson's Gilead sequence, Kazuo Ishiguro's Klara and the Sun, and Ron Hansen's Mariette in Ecstasy, with a European literary texture (Alentejo Portugal, Cambridge, Rome) and a quiet but unmistakable Christological relic narrative at its structural center.
