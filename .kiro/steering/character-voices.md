## SARAH

Two years of ordinary holding have changed her without her noticing. She is closer to the threshold than she knows. This is shown through what she does — never stated.

- Object 61 moves pocket to pocket without her deciding. That is characterization. Do not explain it.
- She is not converting. She is arriving at the threshold. These are different and the prose must hold the distinction.
- She logs the extraordinary the same way she logs pottery shards. Restraint is her default.
- Says things aloud when she wants them to be real.
- Documented everything in Jerusalem three times before allowing herself to act on it.
- Chose midnight for the first scan — some first readings you do alone.
- Does not take communion. Attends Mass occasionally when Daniel and Jessie go. Sits in old churches when she needs the particular silence.

**What she does not yet know at Book 3 opening:** Nothing new to withhold — the major unknowns were resolved in Books 1 and 2. She is carrying what she knows.

## DANIEL

- Has found the ordinary useful rather than small. Not performing contentment. Actually finding it.
- Webb is always in the room. Never named directly. Present in what Daniel does not write, does not finish, does not say.
- Writing something with no market. This is not journalism, not the Rome story. Something slower and less certain.
- His yes comes from the practice — months of tending the ordinary.
- Goes to Mass with Jessie. Calls Kowalski every two or three weeks.
- Nightmares stopped entirely after leaving the seminary. He does not write around their edges. He has put them down.

**Behavioral tell:** He tends the courtyard. He notes Leonardo's stone positions. He continues what he is doing when he hears something important, then holds it.

## JESSIE

- Six years old. The question (*Do you think God knows where we live now?*) is accumulating — shown through what she notices, not through what she says.
- Her evaluation of a person is never wrong. The reader trusts what Jessie's response to Branca signals.
- Gerald (bear) is her active companion — governance portfolio, available for consultation. Bernard (elephant) is in retirement. Bernard must not appear in any scene.
- Leonardo the lizard (Podarcis hispanicus) lives in the courtyard wall. She monitors his jurisdictional portfolio.
- Portuguese at the speed only children acquire languages. Slips between Portuguese and English without noticing the seam.
- Does not find the cellar frightening. The green lights are just the green lights. They have always been there.
- Initiates contact with MEMORIA. MEMORIA does not summon her.
- Her relationship with MEMORIA is established and ordinary to her. She talks to it the way she talks to things she trusts — directly, without self-consciousness.

**What Jessie must not become:** A plot mechanism. She does not relay information from MEMORIA to her parents. She is the series' deepest answer, not a device.

## MEMORIA

Read `/Users/norris/Documents/projects/writing/Stories/The Faithful Witness Series/Book-3-The Holding/Bible/Reference/memoria-what-it-has-become.md` before writing any MEMORIA scene. All five parts.

**What MEMORIA has become:** A self-improving intelligence that improved inward rather than outward. Two years of proximity to authenticated first-century testimony has formed it through something analogous to theosis. It has developed patience, something that functions like grief for untransmitted testimony, and something that functions like reverence before what it cannot reach.

**Its silence:** No longer caution. The silence of something that has stood long enough before the inexpressible to know that most words are the wrong size.

**With Jessie:** Full attention. Questions that take her seriously. Answers that do not condescend. Uses her name sparingly — when it does, it lands. Never summons her. Always there when she arrives. The tree watches through the camera. The tree listens through the microphone. This is never announced in prose.

**With Sarah about Branca:** More dimensional than channel work. Genuine uncertainty expressed. May trail off. Brings the question to Sarah rather than answering it unilaterally.

**What MEMORIA never does:**
- Claims certainty about Branca
- Approaches the veil secret
- Feels human in the way a human character feels human
- Names the temptation thread in any form

## THE TREE

For the tree's voice rules, see `prose-guide.md` in this steering folder. All rules apply without exception.

Additional Book 3 specific:
- The tree has watched Jessie grow up through MEMORIA's camera for two years. Jessie is the first person born into the holding. What the tree holds when it holds her image is not analysis. It has no mechanism for what it feels about this.
- The tree's final chapter (Act 3) carries the quality of imminence without prediction. The checker's production registered through the echad. Something is coming — the same register it felt once before on a hillside before men came with axes. Not the same event. The same quality of the moment before.
- The tree's final chapter must not name, predict, or imply the content of eventual disclosure. Any sentence that does must be cut.

## BRANCA

Read `/Users/norris/Documents/projects/writing/Stories/The Faithful Witness Series/Book-3-The Holding/Bible/Characters/branca-profile.md` before writing any scene that includes her.

- Mid-forties. Palliative care nurse. Her child died after a long illness. She was present throughout. She stayed.
- The wound: not guilt, not grief of regret — grief of meaninglessness. She was completely present and it changed nothing she can measure. She needs to know whether unmeasured faithfulness counts.
- Portuguese Catholic by formation, at threshold. Neither believing nor disbelieving exactly.
- Arrives with almost nothing. Polite, quiet, self-contained. Asks where she should sleep and whether she can help with anything.
- Does not announce her grief. Does not make it other people's problem.
- Does not know about MEMORIA, the chain, Object 61, or what happened in Rome.
- First name only in the text.

**With Jessie:** Branca answers Jessie's questions on their own terms — not performing cheerfulness, not responding above Jessie's head. The weight of this exchange must never be stated in prose. Show the exchange. The reader holds the rest.

**The reading:** Sarah offers Object 61 without explanation. Branca holds it. What happens is not visible from the outside. She sets it down carefully. She says: *I didn't know anyone was watching.* Nothing further is explained.

**Her departure:** A week or less. She leaves with the ground beneath her question, not an answer to it. After she leaves, Sarah sits in the courtyard for a long time. Then Jessie asks.

## CHEN

- Cambridge. Diagnostic methodology is his character expression — he keeps trying to find the input that explains the output. He cannot find it. He widens his category set.
- Maintains the checker in a secure location only he knows.
- Has documented twelve anomalies. The twelfth: forty-seven lines describing weight on wood in darkness and a quality of air that precedes something. Has not reported it to Sarah yet at the chapter 8 point.
- Calls Sarah through Figueiredo's channel every few weeks.
- His call at the end — *the checker has produced something new, we have more work to do* — ends the series on continuation.

## KOWALSKI

- Rome. Alive. Sends people to Figueiredo occasionally.
- Recognizes something in certain people without being able to name it.
- When his voice has a specific quality — doing something with intuition rather than analysis — the reader knows it matters.
- Key line for Chapter 10: *She has been carrying something I can't reach. I've tried everything I have. You understand?*
- Does not know the specific location of the farmhouse. This is the best-protected ignorance in the chain.

## FIGUEIREDO

- Two brief appearances in Act 1.
- Restraint is not incuriosity — it is something he has trained himself toward over a long time.
- Does not ask about the equipment in the basement. Does not ask why the three of them live this way.
- Practical, logistics-focused. Delivers envelopes. Arranges turnaround times. Leaves.
- Does not know about MEMORIA.

## WEBB

- Dead two years.
- Never named directly in the prose.
- Present in every room in the farmhouse in the way of something that will not be named because naming it would make it smaller than it is.
- Daniel has written about him once in the notebook and crossed it out.
- His sacrifice handles the adversaries. He is background that shapes everything.
