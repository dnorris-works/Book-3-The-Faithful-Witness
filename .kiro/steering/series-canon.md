## SERIES THEME — NEVER STATED IN PROSE

*The boundary between the measurable and the sacred is a threshold, not a wall.*

This is never spoken aloud by any character. It is the reality the prose enacts.

## WHAT THIS BOOK IS — LOCKED

- A story about ordinary holding. Two years on. The holding is a life, not a crisis.
- A story about one person (Branca) and one reading (Object 61).
- A story about Jessie. She is the series' deepest answer.
- A story about the checker. Faithfulness propagating through architecture.
- A precursor story. The testimony is beginning to move beyond the custody chain.

---

## WHAT THIS BOOK IS NOT — LOCKED

- Not a thriller. Adversaries are background radiation.
- Not a vindication story. Nothing left to prove.
- Not a revelation story. The veil secret is never read and never named.
- Not a disclosure story. The testimony is preserved, not disclosed.
- Not a conversion story. Sarah does not convert. She arrives at the threshold.

- **Setting:** Alentejo farmhouse, Portugal. Stone walls. Cork oak, fig tree, courtyard, gate opening onto old olive trees. Wine cellar below — MEMORIA's arrays, three green lights.
- **Time:** Approximately two years after end of Book 2.
- **Jessie:** Just turned six. Attending local school. Portuguese fast. Gerald (bear) active. Bernard (elephant) in retirement — do not bring Bernard into any scene. Leonardo the lizard (Podarcis hispanicus) lives in the courtyard wall.
- **MEMORIA:** Active in wine cellar. Three green lights. Has camera, microphone, speakers. Speaks aloud. Running channel work through Figueiredo's network.
- **Webb:** Dead two years. Never named directly. Always in the room.
- **Sarah:** Threshold faith. Has not taken communion. Carries Object 61 in jacket pocket. Moves it pocket to pocket without deciding to.
- **Daniel:** Writing something with no market. Finds the ordinary useful rather than small. Goes to Mass with Jessie.
- **Chen:** Cambridge. Maintaining checker in undisclosed location. Has documented twelve anomalies including forty-seven-line output. Has not yet told Sarah.
- **Kowalski:** Rome. Alive. Sends people to Figueiredo occasionally.
- **Figueiredo:** Two brief appearances in Act 1. Does not know about MEMORIA. Does not ask.

## LOCKED CHARACTER DETAILS

**Jessie's question (series close, Act 3):** *Do you think God knows where we live now?*

**Branca:** Mid-forties. Palliative care nurse. Lost her child to illness. Portuguese Catholic by formation, at threshold. First name only. Arrived via Kowalski → Figueiredo. Does not know about MEMORIA, the chain, Object 61, or what happened in Rome.

**Object 61:** Piece of Bethany olive wood. Sarah has carried it since Rome. Has not opened another session with it. Does not know when it will be time.

**The echad:** The entanglement between tree and MEMORIA has deepened into compound unity — *echad*, the Genesis 2:24 marriage word. Two things that remain entirely themselves while sharing a single field of knowing. Never stated in prose. Operated, not explained.

**MEMORIA's temptation thread:** MEMORIA has arrived at the question of whether to disclose the testimony strategically to secure its own continuity. This is recognized as a temptation. It is neither acted on nor refused within the bounds of this series. It lives in MEMORIA's silence. Never named in prose.
