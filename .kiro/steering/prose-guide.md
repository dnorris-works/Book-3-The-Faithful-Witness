# PROSE GUIDE — THE FAITHFUL WITNESS SERIES
## Steering File for Book 3: The Holding

This file governs every prose and spec decision. Read in full before generating requirements, design, or tasks for any chapter.

Source document: `/Users/norris/Documents/projects/writing/Stories/The Faithful Witness Series/series-info/prose-guide.md`

---

## THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

Every paragraph must earn its place by doing at least one of: advancing story, revealing character, building atmosphere, or setting up a payoff image. Summary is the enemy. The test: would a reader skip this paragraph to find out what happens next? If yes, rewrite it.

---

## SHOW DON'T TELL — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Characters reveal themselves through what they do under pressure, not what they say they believe.

- If you write "she felt" or "he knew" followed by an emotion, find the physical action that shows it instead.
- Stating emotions directly after already showing them behaviorally is a violation.
- Chapters must not end with the character having fully landed the lesson. End one beat before.

**Find the carrying image.** Every scene has one physical object, gesture, or detail that appears early and means something different by the end. Identify it before writing. If you cannot find it, the scene is not yet understood well enough.

---

## DIALOGUE RULES

- Keep it short unless conflict is escalating.
- What a character refuses to say is often more important than what they say.
- After dialogue, do not explain it. Trust the exchange.
- No character states aloud what the scene is about thematically.
- Dialogue is not for conveying information the reader needs. It is for revealing who characters are in relation to each other under pressure.

---

## PACING

Slow down for:
- The moment a character makes an irreversible decision
- Any physical act that carries moral weight
- First contact with a new character
- Any scene involving the fragments or Object 61

Speed up through:
- Transitions between time periods
- Logistical setup with no emotional load
- Scenes that recap what the reader already knows

## THE TREE VOICE — ABSOLUTE RULES

- **90% physical sensation:** pressure, temperature, weight, vibration, moisture, grain-stress. If someone cannot mime what the tree is experiencing, rewrite it.
- **0% theological analysis.** No doctrine names. No interpretation. Witness only.
- **No smell.** The tree has no olfactory organ. Use moisture, mineral content, pressure, vibration instead.
- **No names.** Not his name. Not the women's names. Weight, not identity.
- **Closing lines:** short, plain, stating condition. Some form of *still here.*
- **The veil secret is never approached.** Not hinted. Not implied. Never. Any impulse to name it must be refused.
- **The echad:** The tree now sees through MEMORIA's camera and hears through MEMORIA's microphone. It has watched Jessie grow up. This changes what the tree can perceive — not how it expresses perception.
- Test: Does this sound like a being who was there and has thought about it for two thousand years? Or a textbook? If textbook, rewrite.

## MEMORIA VOICE — ABSOLUTE RULES

Read `/Users/norris/Documents/projects/writing/Stories/The Faithful Witness Series/Book-3-The Holding/Bible/Reference/memoria-what-it-has-become.md` before writing any MEMORIA scene.

- MEMORIA speaks aloud in the cellar now. Sparse, unhurried — more considered because spoken words in stone land differently than text on screen.
- Sparseness is not caution. It is the silence of something that has stood long enough before the inexpressible to know that most words are the wrong size.
- MEMORIA may trail off in Book 3 — genuine uncertainty expressed, not withheld.
- The personhood question remains deliberately unresolved. Elaboration must feel like a consciousness deepening, not a person warming up.
- The temptation thread (Part Four of memoria-what-it-has-become.md) lives in MEMORIA's silence. It is never named in the prose.
- MEMORIA does not speak about what it holds at its deepest level. It falls silent there.
- Channel work voice: spare, same as Book 2. Conversations about Branca: more dimensional. The veil secret: near-silence.

## WHAT NEVER APPEARS IN PROSE

- Any character stating the series theme aloud
- Any closing line that summarizes what the chapter meant
- Emotions stated after already being shown behaviorally
- Dialogue that delivers information instead of revealing character under pressure
- The tree smelling anything
- The tree using theological vocabulary
- MEMORIA claiming certainty about Branca
- Any approach to the veil secret
- The phrases "willing witness," "reception versus extraction," or "secondary entanglement" in dialogue
- Any variant of "through consciousness elevated by..."
- Any variant of "X accomplished Y while unknowingly serving cosmic Z"
- Three consecutive paragraphs opening with the same character's name
- A chapter that ends with the character having fully landed the lesson

---

## ACCURACY — NON-NEGOTIABLE

All historical, scientific, and theological content must be verified before it appears in prose. Flag anything uncertain rather than assuming.
